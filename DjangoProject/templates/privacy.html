2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account information: email address, username, password (stored in hashed form), and optionally phone number, display name, and profile photo.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, language, pages viewed, search queries, listings clicked, referring URLs, and timestamps.

2.3 Information from other sources

  • Third-party sign-in: if you sign in with Google, Apple, or another provider, we receive the basic profile information you authorize.
  • Fraud-prevention and security partners: signals about suspicious activity, such as known spam IP addresses or compromised credentials.
